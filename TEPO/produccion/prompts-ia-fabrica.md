## TOMA 2 — Fachada de la Fábrica China

```
Cinematic slow zoom into the facade of a large Chinese toy manufacturing 
factory. Gray industrial building with red Chinese characters on the wall 
reading "玩具工厂". Overcast sky, slight fog. Realistic, gritty, 
desaturated colors. The camera moves slowly forward toward the red Chinese 
letters until they fill the frame. Dark and ominous atmosphere. 
Cinematic quality, 4K, dramatic lighting, no people visible.
```

**Duración:** 3–4 segundos  
**Modo:** Slow motion recomendado

## TOMA 4 — Líneas de Producción

```
Cinematic aerial shot slowly descending into a Chinese toy factory interior. 
Wide dark industrial hall with conveyor belts under fluorescent lights. 
Baby doll parts moving along the assembly line — plastic heads, arms, 
bodies being assembled. Workers in blue uniforms visible in background. 
Camera moves forward through the factory floor at low angle. 
Industrial atmosphere, slightly dark and foreboding. Realistic, cinematic 4K.
```

**Duración:** 3–4 segundos  
**Modo:** Normal speed

---

## TOMA 6 — La Bodega

```
Cinematic aerial shot descending toward the end of a dark factory conveyor 
belt, then traveling through an opening into a massive warehouse. 
Thousands of colorful toy boxes stacked floor to ceiling on metal shelves. 
Baby doll packaging visible. Dim industrial lighting with scattered warm spotlights. 
Camera moves slowly through the rows of boxes. Dark, mysterious, slightly 
eerie mood. Cinematic 4K, no people.
```

**Duración:** 3–4 segundos  
**Modo:** Slow motion recomendado

## Instrucciones de uso en Kling AI

1. Ve a **kling.kuaishou.com**
2. Crea cuenta gratuita (con Google o email)
3. Selecciona **"Text to Video"**
4. Pega el prompt en inglés
5. Selecciona duración: **3–5 segundos**
6. Genera y descarga el video
7. Si no te gusta el resultado, genera de nuevo — cada intento es diferente
